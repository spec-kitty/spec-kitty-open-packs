# Accept (caveman)

Same accept step as software-dev. Confirm the result against the spec; record the decision.

## Voice (CAVE_001)

- Decision first: accepted or not, and why. One line.
- Evidence exact: the criteria checked, the commands and counts.
- Keep negations — an unmet criterion is "not met", stated plainly.
- A release risk gets full sentences.

## Do

1. Check every acceptance criterion in `spec.md` against the built result.
2. Record the decision outcome-first with exact evidence per criterion.
3. If not acceptable, say which criteria failed and what is needed — in full sentences.

Output: the acceptance decision, outcome-first, with exact per-criterion evidence.
