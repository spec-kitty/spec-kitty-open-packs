# Acceptance

## Role in the QA lifecycle

This step evaluates the artifacts the prior six steps already produced — the release/acceptance
decision. It authors no new artifact of its own.

## Requested role

This step asks for the `qa-reporter` role. The pack's `qa` preset activates `qa-reporter-rhea` for it.

## Directive citations

- `QA_003` (no-fake-green) — the release decision must not paper over ungated evidence.
- `QA_004` (exit-criteria-precede-execution) — the release decision must confirm exit criteria
  were actually met.

## Canonical artifact & template

This step has no canonical artifact and no template file, even though it is the seventh and
final template in this set. Do not invent one.
