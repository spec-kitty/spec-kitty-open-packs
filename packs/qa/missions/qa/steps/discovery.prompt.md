# Discovery

## Role in the QA lifecycle

This step aliases to the built-in `research` action. It investigates the feature under test and
gathers context, but it produces no QA-specific artifact of its own.

## Requested role

This step asks for the `researcher` role. The pack's `qa` preset activates no profile for it; a
researcher profile such as `researcher-robbie` (built-in) fills it when the operator activates one.

## Directive citation

- `QA_005` (risk-based-prioritization) — risk-based prioritization informs what discovery
  investigates before a strategy is authored.

## Canonical artifact & template

This step has no canonical artifact and no template file. Do not invent one.
